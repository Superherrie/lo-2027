Term 2 · Lesson 21 · PAT lesson

Exam preparation: the prepared composition

PAT: practical assessment task

Start hereSlidesNotesPracticalTest yourselfFinished early?

Start here

Today I will

  • Prepare a light, accurate contour of my composition
  • Plan the order of work
  • Decide where the focal point will be strongest
Think first

If someone else had to finish your exam drawing from your preparation, what would they need on the page?

Words to Know

WordMeaning
ContourThe lines of the outer and inner edges of forms.
Order of workThe sequence in which you build the drawing.
Focal pointThe part of the artwork the eye goes to first.
ResolvedFinished, with every part working together.

Check these words in your Art Theory or Art History booklet.

Notes

Preparing what the brief allows before the exam (the composition and contour lines only); planning the order of work and where the focal point will be.

A good prepared contour

  • Light lines: Easy to cover or erase.
  • Big shapes first: Place the whole group before any detail.
  • Check proportion: Sight and measure before committing.
  • No tone: Tone and media are for the exam.

Order of work

  1. Darkest darks: Set the tonal range early.
  2. Big areas: Block in large areas in your main medium.
  3. Paper element: Fix it once the composition is clear.
  4. Focal point: Most contrast and detail last.
Remember

Prepare the map; save the journey for the exam.

Practical

On your own · about 30 minutes

You need: Exam paper, pencils, reference photos, VJ

Exam preparation: the prepared composition

  1. Place the overall shape of the group lightly on the exam paper.
  2. Sight and correct the proportions.
  3. Draw the contour lines lightly, with no tone.
  4. Mark the focal point in your VJ plan.
  5. Write your order of work beside the plan.
  6. Store the paper flat in your class drawer.

Test yourself

  1. What may be prepared before the exam?
  2. Why keep the lines light?
  3. What goes in the order of work?
Show answers

1. The composition and contour lines only. 2. So they can be covered or erased. 3. The steps from darkest darks to the focal point.

Finished early?

Shapes only (about 10 minutes)

Practise light placement.

  1. Squint at your reference photo.
  2. Draw only the five biggest shapes, very lightly.
  3. Check proportions with a pencil.
  4. Compare with your plan.
