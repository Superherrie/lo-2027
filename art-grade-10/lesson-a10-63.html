Term 2 · Lesson 21 · PAT lesson

Final portrait: refining and self-check

PAT: practical assessment task

Start hereSlidesNotesPracticalTest yourselfFinished early?

Start here

Today I will

  • Give and receive kind, specific feedback
  • Check my portrait against the rubric
  • Refine the focal point, edges and tones
Think first

Is it easier to see what is wrong in your own drawing or in someone else's? Why?

Words to Know

WordMeaning
CritiqueA thoughtful discussion of an artwork's strengths and how it could improve.
Focal pointThe part of the artwork the eye goes to first.
Lost and found edgesEdges that are sharp in some places and soft or disappearing in others.
RefineTo improve something with small, careful changes.
ResolvedFinished and well thought through, with every part working together.

Check these words in your Art Theory or Art History booklet.

Notes

Refining the focal point, edges and tonal range; peer feedback and checking against the rubric.

Two stars and a wish

  1. Look first: Look at your partner's portrait in silence for one minute.
  2. Two stars: Name two specific things that work, with art words (for example, the tonal range in the eyes).
  3. One wish: Suggest one specific improvement, kindly.
  4. Thank and decide: The artist thanks the partner and decides what to change.

Refining the portrait

  • Focal point: Put the sharpest detail and strongest contrast at the eyes or wherever the meaning is.
  • Edges: Let some edges soften into the background so the face is not cut out.
  • Darkest dark: Check that the darkest areas are dark enough.
  • Second medium: Check that it is used for a reason and repeated, so the work feels unified.
Remember

Kind, specific feedback makes good work better.

Practical

In pairs, then on your own · about 30 minutes

You need: Final portrait, PAT 2 rubric, sticky notes, VJ

Peer critique and self-check

  1. Swap portraits with your partner and look for one minute in silence.
  2. Write two stars and a wish on a sticky note for your partner.
  3. Read your own note and choose one change to make.
  4. Check your portrait and journal against each line of the rubric.
  5. Refine the focal point, the edges or the darkest areas.
  6. Paste the sticky note in your VJ and write what you changed.

Test yourself

  1. What is a critique?
  2. What are lost and found edges?
  3. Why check your work against the rubric before hand-in?
Show answers

1. A thoughtful discussion of an artwork's strengths and how it could improve. 2. Edges that are sharp in some places and soft in others. 3. To make sure every requirement is met and nothing is left out.

Finished early?

Squint and fix (about 10 minutes)

One last look with fresh eyes.

  1. Turn your portrait upside down for one minute.
  2. Note anything that now looks wrong in proportion or tone.
  3. Turn it back and fix one thing.
  4. Write in your VJ what you noticed.
