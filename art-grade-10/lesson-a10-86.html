Term 3 · Lesson 16 · Practical lesson

Activity 4: Colour Pencil and Mixed Media

Drawing: graphite, charcoal, line, perspective

Start hereSlidesNotesPracticalTest yourselfFinished early?

Start Here

Today I Will

  • Layer and burnish colour pencil with control
  • Use warm light and cool shadow to build form
  • Test colour pencil with at least two other media
Think first

Is a shadow on skin just a darker version of the skin colour, or could it be blue or purple?

Words to Know

WordMeaning
LayeringBuilding colour with several light layers of colour pencil.
BurnishingPressing hard with a light colour or colourless blender at the end to make the surface smooth and rich.
Optical mixingColours placed side by side or layered so the eye mixes them.
Toned groundPaper that is grey, brown or coloured, so the artist adds both darks and lights.
Warm and coolReds, oranges and yellows feel warm; blues, greens and violets feel cool.

Check these words in your Art Theory or Art History booklet.

Slides

1 / 7

Notes

Colour pencil layering and burnishing, warm light and cool shadow, optical mixing; colour pencil over a watercolour or ink wash, on toned paper and with torn collage.

Colour Pencil Techniques

  • Light pressure first: Several light layers keep the paper's tooth for later layers.
  • Layer in directions: Change direction between layers for an even surface.
  • Warm and cool: Warm colours on the lit side, cool colours in the shadows.
  • Burnish last: Press firmly only at the end, where you want a smooth, rich finish.

Colour Pencil with Other Media

  • Over a wash: A loose watercolour or ink wash first, colour pencil detail on top when dry.
  • On toned paper: Darks and lights both added; the paper is the middle tone.
  • With collage: Torn paper for big areas of colour, colour pencil to blend the edges.
  • White pencil: Highlights on toned or dark grounds.
Remember

Light is warm, shadow is cool, and patience builds colour.

Practical

On your own · about 30 minutes

You need: Colour pencils, white pencil, watercolour or ink, brushes, toned paper, magazines, glue, water jars

Activity 4: Colour Pencil and Mixed Media

  1. Make a swatch page: light layering, burnishing, warm and cool, and two optical mixes.
  2. Lay a small wash or glue a torn collage patch on a second page and let it dry.
  3. Choose a small part of your figure (a hand, a foot, the twist of the torso).
  4. Draw it in your first media combination.
  5. Start it again in a second combination.
  6. Annotate what each combination does well.

Test Yourself

  1. Why should the first layers be light?
  2. What is burnishing?
  3. What does a toned ground let you do?
Show answers

1. To keep the paper's tooth so more layers can be added. 2. Pressing firmly at the end to make the colour smooth and rich. 3. Add both darks and lights, with the paper as the middle tone.

Finished Early?

Cool shadow swatch (about 10 minutes)

Shadows without black.

  1. Draw a small ball in colour pencil.
  2. Shade its light side with warm colours.
  3. Shade its shadow side with blues and violets.
  4. Add a cool cast shadow.
