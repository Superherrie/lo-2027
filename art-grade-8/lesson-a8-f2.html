Words to Know

WordMeaning
Elements of artThe building blocks of every artwork: line, shape, form, value, colour, texture and space.
Principles of designThe ways artists arrange the elements: pattern, rhythm, emphasis, unity, movement, balance, contrast, proportion and variety.
ValueHow light or dark something is.
Cross-hatchingLayers of lines crossing each other to build tone.
Cast shadowThe shadow an object throws onto a surface.
Focal pointThe place your eye goes to first in an artwork.

Find these words in your Art Vocabulary booklet.

Notes

Revise the elements and principles with the vocabulary booklet.

Term 1 words to know

  • Line and shape: Contour, implied, expressive, directional, line weight; geometric and organic.
  • Form and value: Form, 2D and 3D, value scale, gradation, H and B grades.
  • Light and texture: Highlight, mid-tone, core shadow, reflected light, cast shadow; actual and implied texture.
  • Space: Positive and negative space, foreground, middle ground, background, overlapping.
  • Principles: Pattern, rhythm, emphasis, unity, movement, balance, contrast, proportion, variety.

How to use the Vocabulary booklet

  • Find the section: Theory words, history words or practical words.
  • Read the meaning and the example: The example shows how the word is used.
  • Say it in your own words: Write the meaning in one short sentence in your journal.
  • Use it: Point to the word in one of your own journal pages.
Remember

Use the words, and the words become yours.

Practical

In pairs, then on your own · about 45 minutes

You need: Vocabulary booklet, visual journal, pencils

Vocabulary matching game in pairs, then catch-up

  1. With your partner, play the vocabulary matching game (15 minutes, booklets closed).
  2. Swap with another pair and mark it with the memo.
  3. Look up every word you got wrong in the Vocabulary booklet.
  4. Write the meaning of each wrong word in your own words in your journal.
  5. Spend the rest of the lesson finishing any missing journal pages.

Test yourself

  1. Name the seven elements of art.
  2. What is the difference between a core shadow and a cast shadow?
  3. Which principle makes one part of an artwork stand out?
Show answers

1. Line, shape, form, value, colour, texture and space. 2. The core shadow is on the object; the cast shadow falls on the surface around it. 3. Emphasis (creating a focal point).

Finished early?

Word-picture cards (about 10 minutes)

Make three quick picture cards to help you remember tricky words.

  1. Choose three Term 1 words you found difficult.
  2. Draw three small boxes in your visual journal.
  3. In each box, draw a tiny picture that shows the word (e.g. dots for stippling).
  4. Write the word and a short meaning in your own words under each picture.
