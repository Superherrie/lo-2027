Term 1 · Lesson 9 · Self-check quiz

My Career Interests

About 5 to 10 minutes · nothing you tick is saved or sent · back to the lesson

Thabo would love to start his own business, Mia would rather help a friend who is having a hard time, and Aarav enjoys fixing his bike. Each of these matches a different career category. Find out which of the six categories fit YOUR interests.

How to Do It

  1. Read each section and tick every activity you enjoy, or think you would enjoy.
  2. Tick as many or as few as you like. Do not tick an activity just because you are good at it: tick what you enjoy.
  3. Click See my result. You get a score out of 6 for each category, and your top two are highlighted.
  4. Write your top two categories in your LO book, with two careers from each that interest you.
