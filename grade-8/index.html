Sources and references

All lesson text, explanations, case studies, questions, memos, tasks and activities in this book were written for The King's College & Preparatory School in 2027 and are original. Where we quote a law, a definition or Scripture, the words are in quotation marks and the source is listed here. Videos belong to their creators and are linked, not copied.

  • Republic of South Africa (1998) Domestic Violence Act 116 of 1998.
  • Republic of South Africa (2005) Children's Act 38 of 2005.
  • Department of Basic Education (2011) Curriculum and Assessment Policy Statement (CAPS): Life Orientation, Senior Phase Grades 7–9. Pretoria: DBE.
  • Holland, J.L. (1997) Making Vocational Choices: A Theory of Vocational Personalities and Work Environments. 3rd edn. Odessa, FL: Psychological Assessment Resources. (The six career types.)

  • Dweck, C.S. (2006) Mindset: The New Psychology of Success. New York: Random House. (Growth mindset.)
  • Covey, S.R. (1989) The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People. New York: Free Press. (The urgent/important grid, often called the Eisenhower matrix.)
