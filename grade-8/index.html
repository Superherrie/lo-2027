Grade 8 Life Orientation

Every LO lesson has its own page. Use it to catch up when you miss a lesson, to study for tests, or when you finish early.

On every lesson page
  • Start here: what you will learn and the words to know
  • Slides and videos from the lesson
  • Notes: the same as your Handbook
  • Written practice (answer in your Activity Book)
  • Finished early? An extra activity and a quick quiz that marks itself
