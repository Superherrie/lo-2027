Grade 9 Visual Arts

Every art lesson has its own page with the slides, notes and the practical steps. Use it to catch up when you miss a lesson, to prepare for a practical, or to study for theory tests.

Bring to every lesson
  • Your visual journal
  • Your pencils (HB, 2B, 4B, 6B) and eraser
  • Your Art Vocabulary booklet
